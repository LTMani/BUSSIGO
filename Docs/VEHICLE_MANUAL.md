## Mechanical Subsystem Diagnostics & Servicing
- **Dual-Circuit Air Brakes**: 8.5 bar operating pressure; automatic spring-brake emergency lockout if primary pressure drops below 3.8 bar.
- **Engine Thermal Management**: 82°C thermostat opening; 92°C radiator cooling fan activation; automatic engine power derating above 112°C.
- **Wear Degradation Tracking**: 15,000 km oil change intervals; 45,000 km brake lining overhaul; 80,000 km tyre retreading/replacement.
