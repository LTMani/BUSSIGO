## Infrastructure Objects & Features
1. **Prakasam Barrage Bridge**: Historic road bridge over Krishna River connecting Vijayawada and Guntur.
2. **Suryapet 7-Hotel Food Court**: Highway meal break hub for express buses on NH65.
3. **FASTag Multi-Lane Electronic Toll Plazas**: Automated barrier arm lifters, overhead RFID transponders, violation alarms, and manual cash lanes.
4. **Eastern Ghats Mountain Passes**: 12 steep hairpin bends with 14% downward gradients, runaway truck sand escape ramps, and masonry parapet protective walls.
