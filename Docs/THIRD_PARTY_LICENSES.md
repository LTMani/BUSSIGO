## Intellectual Property & Fictional Representations
- **Vehicles**: All bus manufacturer names, vehicle model names, chassis designations, and exterior livery designs featured in default prototypes are fictionalized or original creations inspired by common Indian state-transport and private coach archetypes.
- **Operators**: State transport categories (*Pallevelugu*, *Express*, *Ultra Deluxe*, *Super Luxury*, *Garuda*, *Amaravati*, *Vennela*, *Indra*) are used purely for authentic South Indian cultural homage and regional context. No proprietary RTC logos, trade secrets, or copyrighted trademarks are reproduced without license.
- **Geographic Data**: All road alignments, corridor waypoints, elevation maps, and terminal coordinates are based on open geographic data, publicly accessible spatial models, and synthetic procedural generation.

## Open Source Software Attributions
The following open-source algorithms, mathematical models, and design standards are referenced or implemented within the project:
1. **Intelligent Driver Model (IDM)** (Treiber, Hennecke, and Helbing, 2000) - Traffic simulation car-following dynamics.
2. **MOBIL Lane-Changing Model** (Kesting, Treiber, and Helbing, 2007) - Minimizing Overall Braking Induced by Lane Changes.
3. **Pacejka 94 "Magic Formula" Tyre Model** (Hans B. Pacejka, 1994) - Non-linear tyre slip and friction formulations.
4. **Catmull-Rom & Hermite Splines** - Smooth roadway curve interpolation and lane geometries.
5. **Bidirectional A* Graph Search** - Hierarchical road routing and navigation heuristics.
