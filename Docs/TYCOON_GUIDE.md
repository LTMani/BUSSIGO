# BUSSIGO: Travel Company Tycoon & Fleet Management Guide

## 1. Company Setup & Depots
- **Founding Headquarters**: Establish your transport company in Vijayawada or Hyderabad.
- **Depot Network**: Expand regional depots in Guntur, Warangal, Tirupati, Kurnool, and Visakhapatnam.
- **Depot Facility Upgrades**:
  - *Workshop Bays*: Reduce maintenance turnaround times by 40%.
  - *Automated Wash Plants*: Maintain high vehicle cleanliness scores.
  - *Bulk Underground Diesel Tanks*: Purchase fuel wholesale with up to 12% cost savings.
  - *Driver Dormitories*: Recover driver fatigue twice as fast between shifts.

## 2. Dynamic Economy & Seasonal Surges
- **Sankranti Harvest Festival Surge (January)**: 300% passenger demand surge on routes outbound from Hyderabad towards coastal Andhra Pradesh (Vijayawada, Guntur, Rajahmundry, Visakhapatnam). Dynamic ticket yield algorithms allow up to 1.5x surge pricing.
- **Dasara & Diwali Holidays**: Intercity travel peaks connecting cultural hubs.
- **Maha Shivaratri & Tirupati Pilgrim Rushes**: High-demand charter runs to Srisailam and Tirumala.
- **Double-Entry Financial Accounting**: Real-time tracking of 50 general ledger accounts (fares, parcel tariffs, fuel expenses, FASTag tolls, driver wages, insurance, maintenance, depreciation).
